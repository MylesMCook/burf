## Why this track

115 BPM, F major, instrumental (checked by separating vocals with Demucs:
the vocal stem sits 40–50 dB under the instruments), clean, upbeat but calm
synth-pop. It is a four-bar loop of F – C – Dm – Bb in phrases, with a
pad-only intro and breakdown and a final F chord that rings out, which is
what made a clean 33-second edit possible (see `scripts/music.py`):

| 0:29.2–0:33.5 (bar 14) | 2:47.0–2:51.3 | the final F chord (C → F, a V–I close), faded out |
