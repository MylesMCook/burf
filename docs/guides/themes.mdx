The app comes with four themes (Berth Dark, Berth Light, Midnight and Paper)
and **Match system**, which uses Berth Light by day and Berth Dark when
macOS is dark. Pick one in **Settings → Appearance → Theme**.

## Your own

Add a JSON file to `~/.berth/themes/` on your laptop. It appears in Settings
within 15 seconds, or as soon as you switch back to the app. A theme with a built-in's id (`berth-dark`,
`berth-light`, `midnight`, `paper`) replaces it.

```json title="~/.berth/themes/harbour.json"
{
  "id": "harbour",
  "name": "Harbour",
  "appearance": "dark",
  "colors": {
    "background": "#1d1e22", "foreground": "#e3e4e8",
    "sidebar": "#18191c", "sidebarForeground": "#a9abb3",
    "muted": "#25262b", "mutedForeground": "#8a8c95",
    "border": "#2c2d33",
    "accent": "#2a2b31", "accentForeground": "#f1f2f4",
    "primary": "#e3e4e8", "primaryForeground": "#18191c",
    "card": "#212226", "popover": "#222328", "ring": "#5b5d66",
    "success": "#3fb950", "warning": "#e8a33d", "destructive": "#f0564a"
  },
  "terminal": {
    "background": "#1d1e22", "foreground": "#dcdde2", "cursor": "#e3e4e8", "selectionBackground": "#3a3c45",
    "black": "#1d1e22", "red": "#f0564a", "green": "#3fb950", "yellow": "#e8a33d",
    "blue": "#6d9eff", "magenta": "#c88bf0", "cyan": "#4fc1d6", "white": "#c9cad0",
    "brightBlack": "#6b6d76", "brightRed": "#ff7b70", "brightGreen": "#5fd36f", "brightYellow": "#f5c063",
    "brightBlue": "#8db4ff", "brightMagenta": "#dba8f7", "brightCyan": "#7ad6e6", "brightWhite": "#f4f5f7"
  }
}
```

A theme needs `id` (defaulting to the file name), `colors` and `terminal`;
`appearance` is `dark` or `light`. The full type is
[`Theme`](/reference/plugin-sdk#types).

`warning` is mainly the colour of "needs you": the dot of an agent waiting
for you, and the lamp in the app's drawings. It also marks boxes still
connecting, memory running low and other warnings, so keep it easy to tell
apart from `primary`.

## In a plugin

A [plugin](/guides/plugins) can ship themes as files, listed under
`"themes"` in its `berth-plugin.json`, or add one at runtime with
`berth.addTheme(theme)`. Plugin themes come after yours, so a plugin theme
with the same id replaces yours.

## Chat background

**Settings → Appearance → Chat background** puts a picture behind
conversations, in panes and in zen. Pick one:

- **None**, the default.
- A built-in: **Harbour** (the first-prompt painting, by the time of day,
  and the night under a dark theme), **Dawn**, **Night**, **Open sea**,
  **Fog** or **Chart**.
- **Add…** a picture of your own, or drop one on the section. It is shrunk
  to at most 2560 px and kept in the app's own storage on this Mac, with
  the last eight you used.
- **Generate** one from a prompt. Berth runs Codex's CLI on your Mac with
  your Codex account (`codex exec`, read-only, in an empty folder), shows
  the exact command first, and runs it only when you press Generate. It
  takes 30 to 60 seconds. Without Codex installed and signed in
  (`codex login`) it says so.

Then shape it under **Effects**: a **Look** to start from (Clear,
Frosted, Dithered, Ink, Pixel), then **Tone** (Auto moves the picture into
the theme's light, dark under a dark theme), **Dim**, **Frosted glass**,
**Pixelate**, **Dither** (the picture's colours, or the theme's ink),
**Fit** and position. The preview shows a conversation over it.

The conversation always sits on a sheet of frosted glass, as opaque as the
picture needs for its text to keep WCAG AA contrast, so a busy photo stays
readable. A pane's **⋯** menu has **Chat background…** to come back here.
